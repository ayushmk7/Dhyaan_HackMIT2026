## Per-sponsor notes

### Maximor
- Tier: Platinum (listed as "Prosperous Commerce" → maximor.ai in the site bundle; the challenges doc says MAXIMOR).
- Company: NYC, $9M seed led by Foundation Capital (https://www.maximor.ai/blog/maximor-raises-9m-to-give-cfos-audit-ready-finance-automation-without-erp-rip-and-replace); Aug 2026 claims 35x revenue in 9 months and 98% of transactions completing with no human touch (https://www.intelligence360.news/maximor-grows-revenue-35x-in-9-months-as-it-elevates-finance-from-task-automation-to-full-autonomy/). Hiring new-grad SWE (Python, agent tooling, ERP integration).
- Developer surface: **none public** (checked homepage; trust center at trust.maximor.ai only). Do not plan on a Maximor API.
- Proxy signal: their own Sep 5-6 2026 hackathon judged "AO Usage & Build Process 25%, technical execution/reliability 25%, track alignment/real-world value 25%, demo 15%, innovation 10%" (https://syndicate-by-maximor.devpost.com/). Same "Autonomous Office of the CFO" track language as HackMIT.
- Winning shape: pick ONE workflow (e.g. 3-way invoice/PO/receipt match or bank reconciliation), synthetic messy data with injected exceptions, agent with tools + a memory of past failures + eval loop that shows accuracy going up run over run + a human-review queue. Show the improvement chart.

### Voloridge
- Tier: Platinum. Quant fund in Jupiter, FL; Voloridge Health is their health-analytics/VC arm (https://www.caplight.com/investor/voloridgehealth). Ascend Program 2027 (Jan 11-14, 2027, 20 students, apps close Oct 31 2026; https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/voloridgeinvestmentmanagement/jobs/4328356009) — the fast-track prize feeds this.
