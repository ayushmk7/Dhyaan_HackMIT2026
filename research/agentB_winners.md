## 2. Patterns: what made winners win (12)

1. **Physical, in-your-hands hardware that moves.** Grand prizes disproportionately go to things judges can touch: Shepherd's motorized cane literally pushes you (TreeHacks 2026 GP, https://devpost.com/software/raising-cane); OmNom robot taps an iPad kiosk with a stylus (https://devpost.com/software/omnom-hg16v3); Duet's EEG headset (Cal Hacks 11 1st, https://devpost.com/software/duet-0tbkxe). Even scribes won when they had hardware (WingNote RFID badge).
2. **"Absurd but real" technical stunts** win Most-Technically-Complex and VC fellowships: Freak in the Sheets compiles C to Google Sheets (https://devpost.com/software/freak-in-the-sheets-7jl542); dockerc Docker->single binary in Zig (https://devpost.com/software/dockerc); Unreal EngJam game engine inside Figma (https://devpost.com/software/unreal-engjam). Deep systems work beats another CRUD+LLM app.
3. **Hijack a channel everyone already uses (FaceTime, iMessage, Tab key).** FaceTimeOS: call your Mac (Cal Hacks 12 1st, https://devpost.com/software/facetime-macos-ai-agent); Sunday lives in iMessage group chats (https://devpost.com/software/sunday-94odas); doeve controls your PC over iMessage; OnTab makes Tab the accept key in the browser (https://devpost.com/software/ontab). Zero-onboarding demos feel like products.
4. **The demo is the product: 3-minute pitch on a real object.** Mentra winner at HackMIT 2025 demoed on a half-assembled Target dresser and a LEGO set (https://experience.mcintire.virginia.edu/news/pierce-brookins-earns-top-spot-hackmit-event/). FaceTimeOS team removed clicking, kept keyboard-only, and built hard-coded fallbacks to guarantee a clean demo (https://blog.dylanlu.com/cal-hacks-12/). Kinemo won PennApps by letting judges and peers play against each other (https://devpost.com/software/kinemo).
5. **Ground the AI in real public data.** Dispatch scored priority with open Seattle/NYC 911 datasets (https://devpost.com/software/dispatch-evf2sq); TerraLink queried Google Earth Engine live (https://devpost.com/software/terralink-h5vuel); DriveWise used 22 years of accident data (https://devpost.com/software/drivewise-g09s5y); Chilladelphia mapped real Philly heat. Directly relevant to Voloridge/Elastic/Arrowstreet/GiveCampus tracks.
6. **Narrow, named user with a job to do — not "everyone".** 911 operators (Dispatch), nurses (WingNote, NurseNotes), therapists (TheraMind), beekeepers (BeeMovr, HackMIT 2023), firefighters (PUSHPA), gift officers (GiveCampus's own framing). Judges reward "tells them where to spend Tuesday."
7. **Agents that DO the thing end-to-end (call, pay, book), not chat.** Voider: voice -> price compare -> Visa agent payment in <10s (https://devpost.com/software/voider); Sunday phones restaurants via Vapi; Bloom books rides via Browserbase; Superhuman runs 20+ agents in parallel. Sponsors (Fetch, Anthropic, ElevenLabs) all now say "beyond simple chat/TTS."
8. **Accessibility wins only when the mechanism is novel.** Shepherd (motor steering), Edith (haptics + depth, 4xA100), Iris (on-device), BlinkAI (blink morse), BrailleBot ($15 printer), Lume (highlights the exact button for seniors). Plain "describe the scene for blind users" apps appear every year and mostly do not top the board (see banned list).
9. **Multimodal glasses/wearables with a specific action loop.** aesthetica (Ray-Ban gesture -> outfit -> product via iMessage, YC prize), Bob (Spectacles + Gemini Live for repair work, Most Creative), Talk Tuah (AR social coach, Deepgram + Best Beginner), Mentra handyman (HackMIT 2025). Pattern: glasses + one narrow job + an output channel, not "general assistant."
10. **Human-in-the-loop safety as a feature.** Robosurge: LLM decides intent only, deterministic geometry + safety gates (https://devpost.com/software/we-use-nix); OnTab requires Tab to accept; Maximor's 2026 brief explicitly asks for "appropriate human review when uncertain." Show the guardrail in the demo.
11. **Social/play beats productivity for crowd and judge energy.** kinemo (PennApps 1st), MafiAI, CharAIdes, Blitz, Boogie Battle, Vox Populi (ElevenLabs winner). Multiplayer demos generate the "eyes light up" moment; relevant to Meta's "bringing people closer" track.
12. **Self-improvement / measurable delta shown on screen.** OnTab trained a KTO-RL adapter on user accepts; HackOverflow shows a live observability dashboard of agent calls; Dispatch shows queue re-prioritization live. Judges (and Maximor's brief) want "gets better over runs" made visible with a number.

## 5. Quick implications for HackMIT 2026 idea selection
- Prefer ideas with a physical or channel-hijack demo (hardware, glasses, phone call, iMessage) and one narrow user.
- Data tracks (Voloridge, Elastic, Arrowstreet, GiveCampus) are less crowded than voice/agent tracks and reward a real dataset + one insight; combining two of the curated datasets is the pattern that wins "originality."
- Avoid everything in section 3 unless the mechanism is new.
- Voice sponsors want the voice to *do* something (call, route, pay), with sub-second latency shown live.
- Maximor/Ramp/Warp reward a before/after metric on screen (time saved, errors fixed, cost cut); The Token Company prize is a free add-on if you log LLM cost and show a cache/compression delta.
